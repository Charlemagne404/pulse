Privacy Policy

Pulse is designed to keep analytics collection narrow by default and avoid using the product as an identity layer.

What Pulse collects

  • Page and event metadata
  • Device and browser dimensions
  • Country-level location derived from anonymized IP handling

What Pulse avoids

  • No third-party advertising tracking
  • No fingerprinting
  • No sale of collected analytics data

Operational guidance

Start with a short event list, review payloads after installation, and only expand collection where the extra data is justified and reviewable.

For the current in-app version of this policy, visit /privacy.
